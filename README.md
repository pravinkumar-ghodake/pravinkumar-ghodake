> As an independent polymath operating at the intersection of theoretical mechanics, computational physics, and intellectual sovereignty, my work bridges fundamental wave dynamics with applied frameworks. I build numerical models (FEM, spring-mass chains) to quantify early-stage damage, engineer "elastically invisible" metamaterials, and resist algorithmic erasure. I prioritize open, machine-readable science to ensure a fully transparent, globally accessible record of research.
