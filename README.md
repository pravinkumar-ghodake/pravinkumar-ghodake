> ### 🎯 Mission Statement
> **"All linear systems are alike, but every nonlinear system is nonlinear in its own way."** 
> As an independent researcher at IIT Bombay, my work bridges fundamental theoretical mechanics with applied computational frameworks. I build numerical models (FEM, spring-mass chains) to quantify early-stage damage and engineer "elastically invisible" metamaterials. I prioritize open, machine-readable science to ensure a fully transparent, globally accessible record of my research.
